Antiquarianism

In the 16th century antiquarians studied Greek history using ancient artifacts, manuscripts, and historical sights. This was a hobby reserved the wealthy and they displayed these artifacts in cabinets of curios.Antiquarianism focused on empirical evidence with the goal of understanding the past. Books explaining the origins of genealogy, topography, etymology,and religious rituals.
