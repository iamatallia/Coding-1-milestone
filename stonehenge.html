Stonehenge

In the early 17th century William Harvey and Gilbert North conducted the first excavations at Stonehenge. John Aubrey a pioneer archaeologist mapped Avebury and wrote Monumenta Britannica a survey of early urban and military sites. These were one of the the first sites to undergo archaeological excavations. William Stukeley another great contributer to early archaeology quoted Stonehenge as "the most important of the early forerunners of the discipline of archaeology"
