Excavations of Nabonidus

In the beginning King Nabonidus of the Neo-Babylonian Empire discovered a foundation deposit of the ruler Naram-Sin of the Akkadian Empire from ancient Mesopotamia. Naram-Sin ruled the Akkadian Empire from 2254-2218 BC and was ruler when the Akkadian Empire had their greatest high. King Nabonidus was named the first He lead excavations of the temples of Samas the Sun God, warrior Goddess Anunitu, and the sanctuary that Naram-Sin built and had them restored their former glory. He was first recorded person to try to date an artifact and was only 1,500 years off despite his lack of technology. All of the information of his restorations and excavations were later found written in cunieform.
